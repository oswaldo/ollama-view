Getting Started Guide
  1. Ensure Ollama is running: Make sure you have Ollama installed and running on your machine.
  2. Check Connection: By default, we connect to http://127.0.0.1:11434. You can change this in settings.
  3. Download a Model: Click "Add a Model" or use the cloud icon in the sidebar to download models like Llama3 or Mistral.
  4. Start Chatting: Click "Start Chat" on any downloaded model to open a persistent conversation.
